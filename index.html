ABOUT

Meet me

Hi! I am a Master’s student in Biomedical Engineering at Brown University, where I research computational wrist biomechanics in Dr. Vikas Srivastava’s lab. I also serve as Education Chair of Brown’s Graduate Student Council and as a Teaching Assistant for the Engineering Capstone course and the 3D Vision Lab.

Previously, I studied Bioengineering at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, conducting research in computational biology and engineering education. Alongside my research, I served as President of the Biomedical Engineering Society (BMES), founded BioeHub, taught in the Engineering Seminar program, and worked as a Data Scientist at Abbott.
